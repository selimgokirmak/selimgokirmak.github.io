GSMA MWC Exhibitor List Scraper

Trade fairs are one of the best places to connect with businesses, discover new products, and identify potential leads.

GSMA is one of the biggest trade fair organizers. It hosts many international fairs such as MWC Barcelona, MWC Las Vegas, 4FYN Barcelona, MWC Doha. The exhibitor lists at these fairs are worth gold for business development, sales, networking and competitor analysis.

In this post, I’ll walk you through how GSMA MWC Exhibitor List Scraper works, what kind of data it extracts.


What is GSMA MWC Exhibitor List Scraper?

This scraper automatically collects detailed company information from a specific type of trade fair websites provided by GSMA. It is a no-code scraping tool running on the Apify platform, and it is very easy to use even for non-technical users.

GSMA hosts multiple types of event websites, but some of them share the same templates and structures. This scraper is designed to extract data from one of those types of event websites.
