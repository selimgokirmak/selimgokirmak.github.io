Xporience Exhibitor List Scraper V2

Trade fairs are one of the best places to connect with businesses, discover new products, and identify potential leads.

Xporience is a technology platform that supports trade show organizers with its own event planning technology. It hosts many international fair websites such as GITEX GLOBAL, Dubai International Boat Show, GISEC Global, Fintech Surge. The exhibitor lists at these fairs are worth gold for business development, sales, networking and competitor analysis.

In this post, I’ll walk you through how Xporience Exhibitor List Scraper V2 works, what kind of data it extracts.


What is Xporience Exhibitor List Scraper V2?

This scraper automatically collects detailed company information from a specific type of trade fair websites provided by Xporience. It is a no-code scraping tool running on the Apify platform, and it is very easy to use even for non-technical users.

Xporience hosts multiple types of event websites, but some of them share the same templates and structures. This scraper is designed to extract data from one of those types of event websites.

What Is The Difference Between Xporience And Xporience V2?

Xporience Exhibitor List Scraper scrapes exhibitor listing pages which uses normal pagination. Xporience Exhibitor List Scraper V2 scrapes exhibitor listing pages which uses page scrolling pagination.
