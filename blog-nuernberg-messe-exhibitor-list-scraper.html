How to Scrape Nürnberg Messe Exhibitor Directories?

Nürnberg Messe Exhibitor List Scraper connects to public Nürnberg Messe event pages and extracts the complete exhibitor directory as structured data.

Nürnberg Messe powers some of the largest trade show digital catalogs including events across manufacturing, healthcare, construction, automotive, technology, energy, food & beverage, packaging, and consumer goods industries.

The scraper handles pagination automatically, resolves exhibitor detail pages, and returns a clean CSV, EXCEL, JSON dataset with company profiles, contact information, contact persons, booth assignments, product categories, and social media links.

Typical Use Cases

  • Build lead generation lists from trade show exhibitors
  • Enrich CRM records with exhibitor contact details and booth locations
  • Track competitor presence across multiple industry events
  • Analyze trade show trends and exhibitor demographics

    • Extracted Data Fields

    The scraper extracts the following fields for each exhibitor in the directory.

    Company Name
    Official exhibitor or company name
    Address
    Full street address with city, state, zip, and country
    Website
    Exhibitor website URL
    Phone
    Exhibitor contact phone number
    Email
    Exhibitor contact email
    Social Media
    LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube links
    Contact Persons
    Named representatives with name, email, and title
    Product Categories
    Product categories
    Booth Information
    Booth number, hall, and location details

    How It Works

    Step 1

    Enter the Event URL

    Provide the URL of the Nürnberg Messe event exhibitor listing page.

    Step 2

    Find Exhibitor Listings

    The scraper discovers all exhibitor entries and navigates pagination automatically.

    Step 3

    Extract Exhibitor Profiles

    Each exhibitor detail page is visited and all available fields are extracted.

    Step 4

    Return Structured Data

    Results are compiled into clean CSV, EXCEL, JSON and delivered for download or API access.
