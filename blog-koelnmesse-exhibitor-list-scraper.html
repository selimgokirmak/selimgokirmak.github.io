Koelnmesse Exhibitor List Scraper

Trade fairs are one of the best places to connect with businesses, discover new products, and identify potential leads. However, manually collecting exhibitor information from event websites is time-consuming and often overwhelming. This is where web scraping comes into play — automating the extraction of structured data from websites for analysis, research, networking, and business intelligence.

In this post, I’ll walk you through how a custom-built Exhibitor List Scraper works, what kind of data it extracts, and how you can leverage it for B2B lead generation, market research, event networking, and competitive analysis.


Why Scrape Exhibitor Lists?

Exhibitor directories published on trade fair websites often contain valuable information such as:

  • • Company details (name, address, country)
  • • Contact person info (phone, email, website)
  • • Social media profiles
  • • Hall/stand locations
  • • Product groups and brands

Instead of copying and pasting these details into a spreadsheet, a web scraper can extract all exhibitors in minutes, allowing you to:

  • • Build targeted contact lists for outreach
  • • Analyze competitors and industry trends
  • • Prepare for networking before attending events

About the Koelnmesse Exhibitor List Scraper

The Koelnmesse Exhibitor List Scraper is designed for trade fairs organized by Koelnmesse (Cologne, Germany). Many of their events use a consistent website structure, which makes it possible to automate data collection reliably.

Some of the supported events include:

  • • Anuga (Food & Beverage)
