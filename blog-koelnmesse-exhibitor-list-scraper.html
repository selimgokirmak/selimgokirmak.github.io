Koelnmesse Exhibitor List Scraper

Trade fairs are one of the best places to connect with businesses, discover new products, and identify potential leads.

Koelnmesse is one of the biggest trade fair organization. It hosts many international fairs such as Anuga, Eisenwarenmesse, Kindundjugend, Spoga+Gafa. The exhibitor lists at these fairs are worth gold for business development, sales, networking and competitor analysis.

In this post, I’ll walk you through how Koelnmesse Exhibitor List Scraper works, what kind of data it extracts.


What is Koelnmesse Exhibitor List Scraper?

This scraper automatically collects all company information from the Exhibitor List pages on the Koelnmesse fair websites. It is a no-code sraping tool that running on the Apify platform. It is very easy to use even for non-technical persons.

Koelnmesse has multiple different types of event websites but some of them uses same website templates/structures. This scraper aims to exract data from one of that types of event websites.
